# Owner Weekly Review (Head Quarter)

Who: Head Quarter (Owner), with the Admin and Finance. Length: 45 to 60 minutes, same day every week.
Source: the app's dashboard and KPI board, the Weekly summary (last 7 days, earlier weeks one tap away), Me, Escalations, Me, Business rules, and the "what the app does by itself" page. Figures are read from the app; do not re-type them from memory.

Before the meeting:
1. [ ] Admin and Finance have closed the day for each working day this week.
2. [ ] Everyone has sent their updates; the sync status strip shows no stale or refused entries (or each is explained).
3. [ ] A full company file was made this week (see `BACKUP_AND_RESTORE.md`).
4. [ ] Note the record count in Me so the next restore can be checked.

## Agenda and what to read

### 1. Live jobs against the pilot limit (5 minutes)
* Live jobs now: ___ of 5. Live = material order through handover.
* Any capacity exception requests waiting? A 6th live job needs your approval; an Admin cannot approve it. Approve only with crew, cash and QC capacity in mind.
* Decision: approve / refuse / defer, with reason.

### 2. Model split: AIEC jobs vs Marketplace (5 minutes)
* Count and value of jobs by model (AIEC-run vs Marketplace) on the KPI board and the marketplace comparison screen.
* Marketplace: posts nobody applied to, applications waiting for a choice, jobs given this week.
* Question: is the split what we intended for the pilot?

### 3. Delayed and blocked jobs (10 minutes)
* Each job that is delayed or blocked: stage, days waiting, who must act, reason.
* Me, Escalations: items at level 3 (7 working days or office acted twice). Items on hold or in dispute and their reasons.
* Installation progress is read as "Milestones verified: X of 7". Installation time is an **estimate with start conditions** (14 days standard, 28 days complex; proposed numbers). Do not treat a miss as a penalty to the team unless a start condition was met.
* Decision per item: unblock, reassign, hold, cancel, or escalate to a person outside the company.

### 4. Payments: confirmed vs pending (10 minutes)
* Payments confirmed by Finance from the bank this week versus pending, disputed and refunded.
* Pending older than 3 working days: name and reason.
* Remember there is no gateway or bank connection: every confirmation was made by a person. Ask Finance how the week's confirmations were checked.
* Decision: any stage that should be held until payment is verified.

### 5. Commission due and received (5 minutes)
* Commission ledger: due, received, outstanding, by partner.
* Duplicate-lead commission and referral items waiting for decision.
* Invoicing, GST and TDS treatment is for the CA to confirm; do not change it in this meeting (see `PRODUCTION_READINESS_CHECKLIST.md`).

### 6. Partner eligibility and expiry (5 minutes)
* Licences, accident insurance, BOCW, ESIC and safety-training papers ending in 7, 30 and 60 days.
* Strikes, big-job eligibility, technician grades (trainee, helper, technician, senior) and promotion or appeal items.
* Decision: suspend, warn, or renew; record the reason.

### 7. QC first-pass rate (5 minutes)
* Final and site inspections passed first time this week, and the points that fail most (by lead technician).
* Surprise QC and co-worker check results; repeat failures.
* Question: does any step or checklist need a lift engineer's review?

### 8. Safety incidents (5 minutes)
* Hazards, injuries, near misses, SOS: count, status, who handled, corrective action.
* Daily safety check: days missed.
* Any injury or unsafe-lift matter: confirm the legal reporting duty with a qualified safety or legal adviser (do not rely on the app).

### 9. Approvals waiting on the Head Quarter (5 minutes)
* Two-person approvals, capacity exceptions, rule approvals, Admin's own late work, new Head Quarter phone approvals.
* Backup approver: is one named and still in date (up to 31 days)?
* Clear the list or set a date for each item.

### 10. Rule approvals (5 minutes)
* Me, Business rules: every number still "Proposed, not approved" that is actually in use (for example prices, margins, token, milestones, installation days, serve area). Changing a number requires approval again.
* Approve a number only when the real rate card, a CA, or an engineer has backed it. Otherwise keep it Proposed.
* Serve area: Pune by default. PCMC only after Head Quarter approval.

### 11. System health (3 minutes)
* Me, Connections: any integration that changed status (all not connected at pilot start; a person does those steps by hand).
* Company server (if used): ready check, last backup date, phones not synced for 2 days or more.
* Live app version and any planned release or rollback (see `DEPLOYMENT_AND_ROLLBACK.md`).
