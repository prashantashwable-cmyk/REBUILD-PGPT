## What this changes, and what it does not
* The register and checklist rows now say **reported reviewed — evidence to attach**. They become *Verified* only when the reviewer, date and evidence are filled in.
* **Business numbers in the app** (rate card, margins, 28-day complex target, Pune area, 2 % commission and the rest) are still marked "Proposed — not approved". The Head Quarter approves them inside the app, in **Me → Business rules**, which records who approved them and when. A review outside the app does not change that record.
* Any change a reviewer asked for (wording, a checklist item, a tolerance) must be listed here and made in the app as a separate, tested change.
